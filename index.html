A Letter For You

Meri Aashu Jii

Meri sabse pyaari Aashu jii, Jab bhi main ye letter likhne baithta hoon, samajh nahi aata kahan se shuru karoon — kyunki tumhare liye jo feelings hain, unhe words mein daalna waise hi mushkil hai jaise mutthi mein hawa ko pakadna. Phir bhi koshish kar raha hoon, kyunki tum is koshish ki hakdaar ho. Tumhe pata hai, tumse milne se pehle meri zindagi bas chal rahi thi — subah uthna, din guzarna, raat sona. Koi excitement nahi thi, koi wajah nahi thi jiske liye din ka intezaar karoon. Phir tum aayi, aur pata nahi kaise, sab kuch rangeen ho gaya. Chhoti chhoti baatein bhi special lagne lagi. Tumhara good morning text mera din bana deta hai, aur tumhari good night wish ke baad hi neend aati hai. Aashu, tum sirf meri girlfriend nahi ho — tum meri sabse achi dost ho, meri strength ho, aur woh vajah ho jiski wajah se main better insaan banna chahta hoon. Jab bhi main tumhare saath hota hoon, waqt ruk sa jaata hai. Aur jab tum door hoti ho, har minute ghante jaisa lagta hai. I miss you har pal — chahe main busy hi kyun na hoon, tum dimaag ke kisi na kisi corner mein hoti ho. Tumhari smile... woh meri sabse favorite cheez hai is duniya mein. Jab tum haste ho, aisa lagta hai jaise sab kuch theek ho jaega, chahe din kitna bhi bura kyun na ho. Tumhari aankhon mein woh depth hai jisme main kho jaata hoon, aur tumhari care karne ka andaaz — bina kahe samajh jaana ki main kya feel kar raha hoon — woh mujhe har roz tumhare aur qareeb le jaata hai. Main khud ko bahut lucky samajhta hoon ki mujhe tum mili. Itni saari ladkiyon mein se, itne saare logo mein se, tumne mujhe choose kiya, mujh par vishwas kiya, aur mera haath thaam liya. Ye koi chhoti baat nahi hai. Main promise karta hoon ki main is trust ko kabhi tootne nahi dunga. Kabhi kabhi main sochta hoon hamare future ke baare mein — hum dono saath mein ek ghar banayenge, jahan hamari apni chhoti duniya hogi. Subah tumhare saath chai peena, weekend pe kahi ghoomne jaana, ek dusre ke sapno ko poora karne mein saath dena — ye sab sochkar hi dil khush ho jaata hai. Main chahta hoon ki ek din tumhe apna banau, hamesha ke liye, taaki koi bhi humein alag na kar sake. Shaadi ka sapna, ek family banane ka sapna — sab kuch tumhare saath dekhna chahta hoon. Aashu, main tumse waada karta hoon — main kabhi tumhara saath nahi chodunga. Achhe waqt mein bhi, bure waqt mein bhi, main tumhare saath khada rahunga. Jab tum kamzor mehsoos karo, main tumhari strength banunga. Jab tum khush ho, main tumhari khushi mein aur khushi dhoondunga. Tum akeli kabhi nahi hogi jab tak main hoon. Thank you, Aashu — mujhe pyaar karne ke liye, mujhpe vishwas karne ke liye, aur meri life ka sabse khoobsurat hissa banne ke liye. Tumne mujhe sikhaya ki pyaar kya hota hai, care karna kya hota hai, aur kisi ke liye selflessly khush hona kya hota hai. Happy Girlfriend's Day, meri jaan. Tum meri zindagi ka sabse bada blessing ho, aur main hamesha, hamesha tumse pyaar karta rahunga — aaj, kal, aur hamesha. I love you forever, Aashu jii. ❤️
— Tumhara Hamesha ❤️
